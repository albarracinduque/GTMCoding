## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Delta Air Lines (NYSE: DAL) is the world's most profitable airline, generating $61.6B in revenue in FY2024 and serving 200M+ customers across 200+ countries. Despite its global scale, Delta's payment infrastructure shows significant gaps: Elavon (U.S. Bank) is the only confirmed acquirer with no payment orchestrator identified, India-issued credit cards were suspended as of July 2025 with no UPI/Paytm alternative in place, and Delta's LATAM expansion (68% growth in the Delta-LATAM JV) runs with zero local payment methods (no PIX, no Boleto). A simultaneous C-suite sweep effective April 1, 2026 — new President, CFO, COO, and CMO — opens a rare window of influence. Yuno's primary entry point is the India revenue bleed combined with the LATAM APM gap, backed by the greenfield orchestration opportunity.

**Insight #1: The India Bleed — #3 Traffic Market, Cards Suspended, No Alternative**

**Evidence:** Section 1 (India #3 traffic source, confirmed by Semrush) + Section 5 (India-issued credit cards suspended July 15, 2025, per official delta.com advisory) + Section 2 (DAL Global Technology Hub LLP confirmed in Bangalore — Delta is invested in India) + Section 4 (UPI, Paytm, Netbanking: all NOT FOUND on delta.com)

**Pain Point:** Delta's third-highest traffic market cannot purchase tickets using India-issued cards as of July 2025. Delta's own advisory redirects them to use "non-India-issued credit cards" — eliminating millions of potential Indian travelers. No UPI (India's dominant payment at 500M+ users) exists at checkout as fallback.
